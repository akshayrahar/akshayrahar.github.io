MSc Dissertation · Distinction

Advertising & R&D
Carry-Over Effects

Completed at Leeds University Business School — the study examined the carry-over effects of marketing and R&D intensity on firm performance within the U.S. automobile industry. Using a 23-year longitudinal dataset (2000–2023) and fixed effects regression models, the research demonstrated that both marketing and R&D investments significantly enhance firm performance over time, with the most pronounced effects appearing after two years.
