Key Findings
Marketing intensity has a positive carry-over effect for 2 years (β=0.55 → β=0.27) before reversing at t-2 — signalling overexposure diminishing returns.
R&D intensity shows a consistent negative short-term impact on gross profit — investments pay off over extended periods, not immediately.
Fixed Effects model (R²=0.96) outperforms Random Effects, confirmed by Hausman Test — validating firm-level unobserved heterogeneity in the panel data.
