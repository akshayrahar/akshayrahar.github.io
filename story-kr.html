Research & Development Associate

📍 K.R. International Pvt. Ltd. 📅 Sep 2017 – Jun 2019 🏷 Market Research · B2B SaaS · Growth Strategy

Did customer acquisition analysis for an early-stage B2B SaaS product to figure out where growth was coming from and where paid targeting should focus. Identified which customer segments were worth the budget and where the market gaps were.

The Context

This was an early role in a company developing a B2B SaaS product. The product existed but the customer base was thin and the team did not have a clear picture of which types of customers stayed, which ones churned quickly, and which market segments were actually worth pursuing with paid acquisition.

What I Did

  • Pulled customer acquisition data and segmented customers by behaviour, industry, and tenure to understand which groups had the best lifetime value.
  • Identified where acquisition was working and where it was not, giving the team a clearer picture of where to focus targeting.
  • Looked at campaign performance and customer behaviour data together to improve retention metrics alongside acquisition efficiency.
  • Mapped market gaps where competitor products were weak or absent and where the product's strengths could be positioned more directly.
  • Produced analysis outputs that fed into how the team allocated budget across growth initiatives.

What I Learned

This was where I built the habit of working backwards from customer data before making targeting decisions. Not "who should we target?" but "who has actually stayed and why?" The two questions produce very different answers, and the second one is usually more useful.

It also gave me early exposure to the gap between what a marketing plan assumes and what customer data actually shows — a gap I have been looking for ever since.
