Research Assistant

📍 Leeds University Business School 📅 Aug 2024 – Mar 2026 🏷 Analytics · GA4 · SQL · Tracking

Used GA4 and SQL to analyse behavioural datasets, trace user journeys end to end, and find where tracking was breaking. The goal was to make the performance data actually trustworthy before anyone acted on it.

The Work

The role sat inside an academic research context but the problems were the same ones you find in any analytics team: data that does not match, events that fire wrong, and reporting that looks clean on the surface but has gaps underneath.

  • Pulled GA4 event-level data and combined it with SQL queries to find where conversion signals were dropping off or misfiring.
  • Traced full user journeys across acquisition and conversion stages to identify exactly where measurement gaps sat.
  • Proposed structured fixes for broken tracking events and inconsistent dataLayer implementations.
  • Built analysis frameworks to evaluate behaviour across multiple touchpoints, so patterns were readable across channels rather than siloed by source.
  • Wrote up findings in plain language for non-technical stakeholders. The outputs fed directly into channel and content decisions.

Key Insight from the Work

The biggest recurring problem was not missing data — it was data that looked complete but was not. Sessions were being attributed to the wrong source because UTM parameters were being dropped on redirect. Events were firing twice because of duplicate GTM tags. Neither problem was visible in standard reports. You only found them by looking at the raw event stream.

That pattern — data that looks fine until you check it properly — is what I now start with on any new analytics engagement.
