Ran Google Ads and Meta campaigns for e-commerce and B2B clients from scratch, fixed broken GA4 tracking, and built dashboards that replaced manual reporting. Worked independently across multiple accounts and time zones.

The Brief

Clients came with different problems. Some needed campaigns built from the ground up. Others had campaigns running but no idea whether the data behind them was reliable. A few were spending on ads without any dashboards to show results. The common thread was that performance data and campaign decisions were not connected.

What I Did

  • Built Google Ads and Meta accounts end to end for new clients: campaign structure, keyword strategy, audience segmentation, bid logic, budget pacing.
  • Rebuilt a struggling e-commerce account: added 200+ negative keywords, restructured ad groups, switched bidding strategy to Smart Bidding, reallocated budget toward high-intent campaign types.
  • Managed Google Shopping campaigns via Merchant Center: kept feed health clean, segmented by product category, adjusted bids at category level.
  • Audited GA4 and GTM setups for multiple clients. Found misfiring events and conversion tags not recording. Fixed them and recovered conversion data clients did not know they were losing.
  • Ran A/B tests on copy, creatives, landing pages, and audiences on a rolling basis.
  • Built Looker Studio and Power BI dashboards that brought all channel data into one place, live.

How the GA4 Audit Worked

For one e-commerce client, reported conversions in Google Ads were not matching purchases in GA4. I went into GTM and found two issues: the purchase event was firing on the thank-you page but the trigger was set to fire on all pages, and the transaction ID was not being passed correctly so deduplication was failing. Fixed both, reprocessed 30 days of data, and recovered roughly 18% more conversions than had previously been attributed to paid channels.

This changed the client's view of which campaigns were actually working. Two campaigns they had been about to pause were performing well. Two they had been scaling were not.
